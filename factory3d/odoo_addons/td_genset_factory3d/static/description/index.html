Generator Factory Bridge

Link of Generators with the factory model: telemetry, maintenance requests, 3D card, blackout balance

What it does

Bridge between Generators and the Factory 3D suite: telemetry, energy, maintenance and a 3D card for every generator.

Key features

  • Generator signals in telemetry
  • Maintenance requests for generators
  • Generator card in the 3D viewer (optional)
  • Power balance during a blackout in Factory Energy

Requirements

  • Works on Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise; no Enterprise module is required.
  • Factory 3D suite modules: Generators (td_genset), Factory 3D Core (td_factory3d), Factory 3D Telemetry (td_telemetry), Factory Energy (td_energy).
  • Interface language: Ukrainian; an English translation is not included yet.

Support
