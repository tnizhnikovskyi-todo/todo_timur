Fire Safety Access Bridge

Evacuation doors in the access control system and release of drill locks

What it does

Bridge between Fire Safety and Access Control, installed automatically when both are present. It adds no models of its own.

Key features

  • Emergency exit doors EX-* in the access control system: the drill report shows who left by an exit other than the one assigned to their zone route
  • After a training alarm ends, the doors unlocked by the alarm are locked again and the fire alarm of the doors is acknowledged; a real alarm is never touched

Requirements

  • Works on Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise; no Enterprise module is required.
  • Factory 3D suite modules: Fire Safety (td_fire_safety), Access Control (td_access).
