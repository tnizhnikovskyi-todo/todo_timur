Fire Safety 3D Bridge

Viewer providers: fire and evacuation sections

What it does

Bridge between Fire Safety and the 3D viewer, installed automatically when both are present. It adds no models of its own.

Key features

  • Fire section in the 3D viewer
  • Evacuation section in the 3D viewer

Requirements

  • Works on Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise; no Enterprise module is required.
  • Factory 3D suite modules: Fire Safety (td_fire_safety), Factory 3D Viewer (td_viewer3d).

Support

Part of the Factory 3D suite for Odoo 19. Questions and support requests: support@todo.ltd
