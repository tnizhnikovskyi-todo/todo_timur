Energy Resources

Gas, heat, compressed air and water: meters of any resource, 15-minute series, cost, savings reserves

What it does

Extends Factory Energy to non-electric resources: gas, heat, compressed air and water, with prices, metering points, meters and savings reserves.

Key features

  • Resource catalog, prices, metering points and meters
  • 15-minute intervals: meter value, flow integral and estimate
  • Reactive energy
  • Usage summary, savings reserves and detectors
  • Energy Resources screen and Excel export

Requirements

  • Works on Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise; no Enterprise module is required.
  • Factory 3D suite modules: Factory Energy (td_energy).
  • Python packages: xlsxwriter.
  • Interface language: Ukrainian; an English translation is not included yet.
