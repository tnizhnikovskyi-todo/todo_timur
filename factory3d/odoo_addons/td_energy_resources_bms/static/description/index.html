Energy Resources BMS

Bridge of Energy Resources and Building Management: heat points and meters from BMS, KPIs, three detectors

What it does

Bridge between Energy Resources and Building Management, installed automatically when both are present.

Key features

  • Heat points and meters created from the building management system
  • Temperature and heat-recovery KPIs
  • Three savings-reserve detectors

Requirements

  • Works on Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise; no Enterprise module is required.
  • Factory 3D suite modules: Energy Resources (td_energy_resources), Building Management (td_bms).
  • Interface language: Ukrainian; an English translation is not included yet.

Support
